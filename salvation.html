THE GOOD NEWS

Hope through Jesus Christ.

The message of the Bible is the good news that God saves sinners through Jesus Christ.

The gospel

01

Our need

The Bible teaches that all people have sinned and need God's forgiveness (Romans 3:23).

02

God's love

God demonstrated His love for us through the death of Jesus Christ (Romans 5:8).

03

Christ's resurrection

Jesus died for our sins, was buried, and rose again (1 Corinthians 15:3–4).

04

Respond in faith

God calls us to repent and put our faith in Jesus Christ (Acts 3:19; Ephesians 2:8–9).

If you have questions about salvation or would like to talk with someone, please contact the church.
