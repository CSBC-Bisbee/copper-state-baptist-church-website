Our statement of faith

This page is a place for the church's official statement of faith. The wording below is a placeholder and should be replaced with the congregation's approved doctrinal statement before launch.

01

The Bible

We believe the Scriptures are God's inspired Word and our final authority for faith and practice.

02

God

We believe in one true and living God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

03

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully man, who died for our sins and rose bodily from the dead.

04

Salvation

We believe salvation is God's gift of grace, received through faith in Jesus Christ.
